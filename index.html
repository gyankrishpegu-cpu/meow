The traditions

Eight states.
One thread.

The Northeast is home to hundreds of communities and as many ways of adorning the body. We don’t try to flatten them into one look — we follow the thread that runs between them: beads passed down, metal worked by hand, colour worn with pride.

Signature piece

Inspired by Tripura

Silver Coin Collar

The first sketch in the Samavaya notebook. Thirteen coins, each hung from a tiny trefoil drop, swing from a collar of red glass beads — a tribute to the coin necklaces Reang women have worn for generations.

  • Coins polished by hand
  • Strung on silk-core thread
  • Adjustable 16 – 20 in
  • Arrives in a handwoven pouch

How it’s made

    Sketched by hand
